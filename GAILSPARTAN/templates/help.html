Guidance and safety

Using GAILSPARTAN

A short guide to getting useful, source-grounded answers.

Ask better questions

  • Ask about a specific policy, deadline, process, or requirement.
  • Upload the relevant PDF with the question when it is not indexed yet.
  • Open the source information shown under an answer before acting on it.

Know the limits

  • GAILSPARTAN does not browse the web or call external AI services.
  • Image-only PDFs may need OCR and may not be searchable.
  • If no evidence is found, confirm the policy with the responsible team.

Privacy and demo data

This version is designed for local development and public sample documents. Keep confidential, personal, regulated, and production organizational data out of the demo.
